1. Understand the Problem

What we are trying to solve

🍽️

Food insecurity

Some people do not have regular access to enough safe and nutritious food.

💰

Affordability

Healthy food can become difficult to afford when household budgets are limited.

♻️

Food waste

Extra food from homes, events and institutions can be wasted instead of being shared.

Design challenge: How might we make food support easier to find while helping communities share safe surplus food?

2. User Research

Research has started — more responses will be added as we test the idea.

👨‍👩‍👧 Families

Need affordable food and reliable access to basic nutrition.

🎓 Students

Can help through awareness, volunteering and responsible food sharing.

👨‍🌾 Farmers / sellers

May have produce or food that needs a better route to local communities.

Research status: Initial user needs identified. The survey below is now being used to collect feedback before the next prototype version.

5. Define

Turning research into a focused user need

Problem statement

People facing food insecurity need a simple way to discover available food support, while people with safe surplus food need an easy way to share it locally.

How Might We?
How might we connect food support, donors and volunteers through a simple community experience?
