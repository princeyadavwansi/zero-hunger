Our Project Goal

To understand people's food-related challenges and develop ideas that can improve access to safe, nutritious and affordable food.

1. The Problem

What are we trying to understand?

🍽️

Food Insecurity

Some families do not have regular access to enough nutritious food.

💰

Affordability

Low income can make healthy food difficult to buy regularly.

♻️

Food Waste

Food can be wasted while other people face shortages.

Initial problem statement: “How might we help people get regular access to affordable and nutritious food while reducing unnecessary food waste?”

2. User Research

Early findings from our research stage

👨‍👩‍👧 Families

Need affordable food and stable access to basic nutrition.

👨‍🌾 Farmers

Need better ways to connect produce with consumers and reduce losses.

🎓 Students

Can contribute through awareness, food-sharing and community activities.

Research status: Initial user needs have been identified. Interviews/survey data will be added to the website in the next stage.

3. Define

Turning observations into a clear design challenge

Our Design Challenge

People facing food insecurity need a simple and accessible way to find affordable nutritious food and connect with available community resources.

How Might We?
How might we make nutritious food more accessible while encouraging communities to reduce food waste?

4. Ideate

Possible solutions we are exploring

📱

Food Connect

A platform to connect extra food from homes, messes and events with people who need it.

🏪

Local Food Support

Map nearby low-cost food resources, community kitchens and support centres.

🌾

Farm-to-Community

Explore ways to connect local farmers with communities and reduce food loss.

5. Design Thinking Process

Our project follows the Double Diamond approach

01

Discover

Research users and understand the problem.

02

Define

Identify the main user need.

03

Develop

Create and compare possible ideas.

04

Deliver

Prototype, test and improve the solution.
