01 · Understand

Two problems. One community opportunity.

Food insecurity and avoidable food waste can exist side by side. Our concept explores a safer, simpler way to connect people and resources.

🍽️

Access to food

Individuals and families may need help finding reliable, affordable food support in their area.

♻️

Surplus food

Homes, events and food businesses can sometimes have safe extra food that could be shared instead of wasted.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Local connection

Volunteers and community organisations can help bridge the gap when coordination and food-safety checks are in place.

Design challenge: How might we help communities discover food support and share suitable surplus food responsibly?

03 · Design Thinking

From understanding to testing.

We use the Double Diamond approach to explore the problem before deciding how to improve the solution.

01 · Discover

Listen

Explore food waste, food access and the needs of different community members.

02 · Define

Focus

Define the key challenge: making local food support easier to discover and coordinate.

03 · Develop

Explore

Build and compare ideas such as listings, request forms and volunteer support.

04 · Deliver

Test & improve

Use feedback to improve the prototype. Real-world use needs verified partners and safeguards.

Problem statement: People who need food support need a clearer way to discover local options, while donors need a responsible route for offering suitable surplus food.

FAQs

What this prototype can—and cannot—do.

Are these real food donations or requests?

No. Entries are only demo listings saved in your browser. They do not reach food banks, NGOs, donors or people seeking support.

Will entries appear on another person's device?

No. This version uses local browser storage. A shared service would require a secure backend and appropriate access controls.

How does this relate to Design Thinking?

The page presents the problem, a defined user need, a proposed solution, working prototype journeys and a feedback form. Feedback can inform future improvements.

What would be needed before a real launch?

Partnerships with verified organisations, safe-food handling rules, secure data storage, location-aware matching, moderation, and a process for confirming and completing requests.
